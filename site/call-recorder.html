Two ways to record

Use whichever fits the day

Set up — your merge test is still to do

1   Three-way merge — on your TELUS phone

Best for calls you place. The recorder is configured for this route, but your own merge test hasn’t been done yet — it’s a bonus check, since the system already proved itself without it, so there’s no rush on a bad-focus day.

  1. Call the person as a normal call and wait for them to answer.
  2. Tap Add Call, dial 778-752-9955.
  3. Tap Merge. The recorder has now joined silently.
  4. Talk for 30 seconds or more so both voices are captured, then hang up as usual.
Good test partner: Fo at +1 415-312-5395 — a real voice on the other end, no clinic bothered.
Better shape — one step left

2   Softphone — dial straight through Telnyx

No Add Call, no Merge. The call lives on Telnyx from the first second, so recording is simply on. It also sets up the inbound fix as a planned phase two: your cell forwards to 778 and Telnyx rings the app instead of your cell number — that’s what avoids the loop. That inbound bridge isn’t built yet.

  1. Install the free Linphone app (Zoiper also works).
  2. In the Telnyx portal, open SIP Trunking → jas-softphone and set your own password. The temporary one was random on purpose — your real password never passes through anyone else.
  3. On that same screen, turn Recording on for all calls if you see the setting.
  4. Sign in to Linphone with the details in the card below.
Honest trade-offs: calls ride your Wi-Fi / data, callers see 778-752-9955 instead of your 604 number, and the app has to be running to ring. Keep your TELUS line in mind for 911 — on a plan-less phone dispatch can’t call you back if the call drops.
